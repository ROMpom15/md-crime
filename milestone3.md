## 1. Introduction and Problem Statement

* **1.1. Introduction:** Provide context for the project domain and its significance.

The introduction of the coronavirus to the United States in 2020 led to an increase in crime rates across the country. This project heavily focuses on how crime rates changed on college campuses during the height of the COVID pandemic. 


* **1.2. Problem Statement:** Clearly and specifically articulate the problem your project addresses. Why is this problem important? What are the existing limitations or pain points?

College campus crimes are a continuous concern for law enforcement and families across the United States. In order to implement a change to decrease criminal activity on college campuses, it must first be understood why these crimes are occurring and what trends can be identified. Current research suggests an increase in crimes between 2020 and 2022 across the United States. Unfortunately, statistiscal research varies across research sources. 

This project aims to pinpoint crime trends on campuses between 2018 and 2023 in order to suggest a potential solution to decrease criminal activity and make campuses safer.

* **1.3. Proposed Solution:** Briefly introduce your system/approach as the solution to the stated problem.

Team 7 is proposing a scalable crime pattern analysis framework. This system is designed to efficiently process historical campus crime datasets from the Department of Education and generate predictive insights into crime patterns.
